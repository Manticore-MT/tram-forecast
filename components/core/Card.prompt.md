The workhorse container for facts, tracks, prizes, experts and dashboard panels.

```jsx
<Card tone="surface" interactive>
  <div className="mt-eyebrow">Трек 02</div>
  <h3>ИИ-прогноз загрузки трамвайных маршрутов</h3>
</Card>
```

`pin` applies the brand's teardrop radius — use it for at most one or two hero cards per page, never for a whole grid. `glass` needs something behind it (map, photo, gradient) or it reads flat.
